## r/ShowYourApp and r/sideprojects

**Title:** I launched NowGo: what's on in NYC tonight, and when you have to leave to make it

**Body:**

It's 7pm, you want to go out, and you end up with six tabs open trying to
work out what's on and whether you can still get there. NowGo puts it all in
one list.

- Tonight's concerts, jazz, comedy, theatre and sports across the city, usually 150+ events
- A leave-by time for each one from where you are, by subway, on foot or by car
- Which rooms let you walk in without a ticket, checked venue by venue
- A Tomorrow tab, for when tonight is winding down
- Surprise Me: picks something starting soon that you can still make

It's free, iPhone only, NYC only. It's been in beta with friends since the
summer and went live on the App Store this week.

https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nowgo-nyc/id6797667109

Solo project: React Native/Expo app, Node and Postgres backend. Happy to talk
about any of it, and I'd love to hear where it gets something wrong.
